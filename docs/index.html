Bradley Analytics Software Engine

An NBA analytics engine built in Python and R, transforming raw shooting data into publication-ready basketball visualizations.

@bradleyanalytics | Instagram Portfolio

Six engines,
one dashboard

Search by Player

Real shot charts, heat maps, hex shot charts, and animated shot charts for any player and season.

Search by Team

The same court visualizations, built from every shot a team took in a given season.

Search by Criteria

Filter the whole league by position, height, and any combination of stats, then scatter-plot the result.

Trade Machine

Swap players between two real rosters and see the before/after, with optional salary and draft-pick data.

On/Off Stats

How a team's numbers shift with two or three specific teammates sharing the floor, versus their season average.

Advanced Stats

True shooting %, usage rate, offensive/defensive rating, and more, for any player or team.
